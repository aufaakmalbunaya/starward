Emberline — hold the line against the wildfire. A fire is sweeping the forest toward the ranger station, pushed by a wind that shifts every wave. You are the fire crew: dig firebreaks under your feet, splash water on the flames, refill at the pond, and keep the station standing for seven waves. Fire spreads from tree to tree, embers jump ahead of the front downwind, and wet ground holds the line. Between waves, pick one of three upgrades: a bigger tank, faster boots, a wider hose, fireproof saplings, and more. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make the best game" bounty, $1,000 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. WASD or arrows to run, hold Space to dig a firebreak, K or click to splash water, stand by the pond to refill. Press 1–3 on the upgrade screen to pick a card, P to pause, M to mute.
