Emberline — hold the line against the wildfire. A fire is sweeping the forest toward the ranger station, pushed by a wind that shifts every wave. You are the fire crew: dig firebreaks under your feet, splash water on the flames, refill at the pond, and keep the station standing for seven waves. Fire spreads from tree to tree, embers jump ahead of the front downwind, and wet ground holds the line. Three kinds of flame hunt you — fast Ember Imps, spreading Crown Fire, jumping Spot Fires — and chained douses build a score combo up to x8. Between waves, pick one of three boons: a wider nozzle, an ember suit, a rain call, a deep well, and more; boons stack and stay with you. On wave 7 the Crownfire itself walks out of the smoke — douse it to win. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make the best game" bounty, $1,000 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. WASD or arrows to run, hold Space to dig a firebreak, K or click to splash water, stand by the pond to refill. Press 1–3 on the boon screen to pick a card, P to pause, M to mute.
